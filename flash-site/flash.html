Flash your Mini Kenterprise

  1. Plug your Mini Kenterprise into this computer via USB.
  2. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (Web Serial is required - Firefox and Safari won't work).
  3. Click the button below and pick the right serial port.

This installs the latest firmware straight from your browser - no Arduino IDE required. Once it's done, connect to the boat's own WiFi network (default name MiniKenterprise_1) and open http://1.2.3.4 to drive it and change settings (WiFi credentials, pins) later on.
